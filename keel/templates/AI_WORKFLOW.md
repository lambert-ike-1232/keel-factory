# AI Workflow Log: [Project name]

Written continuously, one entry per working session. Cheap to keep, expensive to
reconstruct.

**Why this file exists:** any serious account of the work — a case study, a launch
post, a technical sales conversation, an onboarding doc, or an honest retrospective —
needs to answer the same four questions: what was built, with what, what those tools
caught, and what went wrong. Recording that as you go turns writing it up into a
compile step instead of an archaeology project at the end of an exhausting build.

It is also the most honest record of whether the framework is earning its keep. A
phase that never catches anything should be questioned.
